FUEL LAB supports meal planning and nutrition education. Current example menus, portions and nutrient estimates are not individually validated and are not a personalized nutrition prescription. An imported training schedule does not establish your energy needs or medical suitability.

Before following a plan or changing foods, consult a qualified nutrition professional and, when appropriate, a physician. Check food labels, ingredients and allergens, especially if you have allergies, medical conditions, take medications, or have other special dietary needs. Never change medical treatment based on this app.
